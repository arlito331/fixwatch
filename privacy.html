LogPothole Privacy Policy

PowerFix Inc. · Last updated July 26, 2026

LogPothole (formerly FixWatch) is an internal field tool used by government road staff and PowerFix crews to report potholes — a photo and an exact location — and to document their repair: when a pothole was fixed and how the repair holds up over time in photos.

What the app collects

  • Location — only when you tap "Use my current location" while reporting a pothole, to place the report's pin on the map. The app does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos — only the photos you choose to attach to a report or its repair timeline, from your camera or photo library.
  • Report details you type — labels, dates, notes, city and country of the pothole.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, and no collection of names, emails, or contacts.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No background location tracking.

Where data is stored

Pothole reports and photos are stored in a PowerFix-controlled repository on GitHub (GitHub, Inc.), which serves as the team's shared database. The team access code you enter is stored only on your device.

Your choices

Location and photo access are optional and requested only when you use those features; you can decline in iOS Settings at any time. To request removal of a photo or record, contact us and we will delete it from the repository.
